# Mathematical references

1. **Arseniy V. Akopyan.** *Дискретные трансверсали выпуклых множеств*
   (*Discrete transversals of convex sets*). Candidate of Sciences
   dissertation, Moscow, 2010, 75 pages; defense at P. G. Demidov Yaroslavl
   State University. The original Challenge identifies the approximation
   results as Theorems 3.7, 3.17, and 3.18 in Chapter 3.
   [Russian State Library record](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01004895074).
   This is the 2010 source named in the original theorem's documentation;
   it is distinct from the earlier preprint below.

2. **Arseniy V. Akopyan.** *A PL-analogue of the Nash–Kuiper theorem*.
   Preprint, 2007, in Russian.
   [Author's publication list](https://users.mccme.ru/akopyan/papers.html),
   [preprint](https://www.moebiuscontest.ru/files/2007/akopyan.pdf).

3. **Arseniy V. Akopyan and Aleksey S. Tarasov.** *A Constructive Proof of
   Kirszbraun's Theorem*. Mathematical Notes **84** (2008), 725–728.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1134/S000143460811014X),
   [author-hosted paper](https://users.mccme.ru/akopyan/papers/Kirszbraun%20Akopyan%20Tarasov.pdf).

4. **Ulrich Brehm.** *Extensions of distance reducing mappings to piecewise
   congruent mappings on* ℝᵐ. Journal of Geometry **16** (1981), 187–193.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01917587).

5. **Anton Petrunin and Allan Yashinski.** *Lectures on piecewise distance
   preserving maps*. 2014, version 3, 4 November 2014.
   [arXiv:1405.6606v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.6606v3).
   Background for finite extension, folding, and PL approximation.

The current target is the original finite-source statement, with the exact
spherical alternative of uniform strict shortness or image containment in
an **open** hemisphere. The complete proof in this project transports the
supplied alternative formal development to that original statement; it does
not identify the two different polyhedron definitions without proof.
